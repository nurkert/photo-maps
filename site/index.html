PhotoMaps

Your photos on Minecraft maps

Craft a photo code, scan it with your phone, hang your photo on the wall. For Paper and Spigot, Java and Bedrock.

Download Getting started

Minecraft 1.21.4+ · Paper · Spigot · Geyser

How it works

  1. 1

    Craft a photo code

    →

    Four ink sacs around an empty map.

  2. 2

    Scan it

    Right-click it and it becomes a map with a QR code. Scan it with your phone camera.

  3. 3

    Pick a picture

    Take a photo, a selfie or pick one from your gallery. Choose the size, move, zoom and leave squares out.

  4. 4

    Hang it up

    The maps land in your inventory. Put one into a frame and the others hang themselves next to it.

Features

Feels like Minecraft

The page on the phone looks like a Minecraft menu: option sliders, buttons, tooltips and toasts.

Java and Bedrock

The photo code has its own texture on both editions. The resource pack and the Geyser mapping install themselves.

Encrypted uploads

HTTPS with TLS 1.2/1.3, Let's Encrypt certificates or a self-signed one, expiring codes and a strict security policy.

Real map colours

Pictures are dithered into the map palette, and the phone shows a preview of exactly how they will look.

Hangs itself

One click on a frame and every part of the picture finds its place, respecting WorldGuard and other protections.

Survival friendly

Every map of a picture costs an empty map. Pictures survive restarts and can be copied like any map.

Ready to try it?

Drop the jar into your plugins folder and start the server. No database, no extra web server, no setup for LAN servers.
